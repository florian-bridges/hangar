# Close-out

Ends the project. Prepared when the last milestone of the project plan is accepted, or when the owner stops the project.

## Agenda

1. **Results against the goals** of the project plan.
2. **Open items:** open findings, TBDs, unverified requirements, open issues; each closed, transferred or accepted as open.
3. **Lessons learned:** what worked and what did not, for the method (agents, meetings, shift) and for the PLM tool.
4. **Decision:** closed, or closed with open items.

## Outcome

* The final baseline on `main`, tagged `final-baseline`.
* Lessons learned as issues on https://github.com/florian-bridges/hangar (method) and https://github.com/florian-bridges/PLM (tool).
* Open issues closed or labelled `won't do`.
