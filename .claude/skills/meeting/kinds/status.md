# Status meeting

On the owner's request: progress, risks and what comes next. No decision is prepared; one is taken only if the owner raises it.

## Agenda

1. **Progress:** milestone, entry conditions closed and open, windows used.
2. **Since the last meeting:** merged work packages, with IDs.
3. **Risks and blockers:** `risks.md` and the open `blocked` issues.
4. **Coming decisions:** what the owner will be asked, and when.
5. **Questions from the owner.**
