# Re-plan

The milestone is over budget, has stalled, or its scope no longer fits. Prepared when the runner reports OVER BUDGET or stops for a stall. Only the owner sets a budget.

## Agenda

1. **Where we stand:** windows used against the budget, entry conditions closed and open (from `timeline.md` and the runner's progress line).
2. **Why:** what took longer or was not foreseen, in a few lines.
3. **Options:** cut scope (which entry conditions, with their effect), a new budget (how many windows and why), or stop the project.
4. **Recommendation.**
5. **Decision.**

## Outcome

The new budget and scope in `timeline.md`, stated as the owner's decision with the MTG ID.
