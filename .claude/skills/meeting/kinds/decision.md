# Decision meeting

One or more owner decisions that block work or have piled up as `needs-owner` issues. Prepared when a blocking decision is open, or when three or more are open.

## Agenda

Per decision, in order of the work it blocks:

1. **Question** and why it needs the owner (trade-off, relaxed requirement, owner constraint, budget).
2. **Options** with consequences in numbers (cost, mass, margin, schedule in windows, risk), from analyses or decisions by ID.
3. **Recommendation** and the reason.
4. **Decision.**

## Outcome

One `DEC-…` per decision, or the owner-constraint requirement changed; the answered issues closed.
