# Milestone review

The owner accepts a milestone of the project plan (e.g. *System design*, *Sim ready*). Prepared when its work is done and the independent review items for its scope are written.

## Agenda

1. **Summary:** what the milestone was to deliver and what was delivered, with IDs.
2. **Reviews:** verdicts and open findings; no open `major`.
3. **Usage and risks:** windows used against the budget; the risks in `risks.md` that changed.
4. **Open owner decisions** of the milestone.
5. **Verdict:** accepted, or rework (with the rework as issues).
6. **Next milestone:** entry conditions and budget.
